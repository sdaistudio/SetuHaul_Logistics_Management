One exception, end to end

A promise is made only after one transaction books the slot. Every path either asks one question, escalates with options, or books through one checked transaction. Pick a case to watch its path.

Adapted from the solution design: there, a pick takes a 3-minute hold and only "YES" books. Here the pick itself is the booking request, checked in one transaction — one fewer message for a driver on the road. A hold exists only while a coordinator decides.
