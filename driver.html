● Seven languages

Drivers can write in any of seven languages

English, Hinglish and Hindi were there from day one; Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi and Marwari are new. Same engine, same checks, same booking in every one. These conversations were run through the real workflow on the 4 August data (scripts/language_demo.py); the right column is the same conversation sent in English, so you can see the meaning and that the outcome is identical. Reply texts in the new languages are drafts for review by a native speaker.

setuhaul/languages.pyNew: the language list, scripts (Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Devanagari), native digits, marker words that tell Marathi and Marwari from Hindi, and the reading vocabulary per language (o'clock words, durations, late words, "after", ordinals, yes/no, safety words).
